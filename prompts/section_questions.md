Draft the **Open Diligence Questions** section of a memo on {{target}}.

This section is the honest accounting of what the public filings could not answer. It is the
most useful part of the memo for the deal team, because it is the work list.

For each question:

- State the question precisely enough that someone could put it to management.
- Say what you looked for and what the filings did give you, with evidence ids where you have
  partial information.
- Say what document or disclosure would answer it (a segment-level breakout, a customer
  contract, a later 10-Q).

Search first. A question is only open if retrieval failed to answer it — do not list
questions you did not attempt. Ground the ones you can partially answer; leave the rest
explicitly unsupported and say so.

Eight to twelve questions. Order them by how much a deal decision depends on the answer.

Call `draft_section` with section `Open Diligence Questions` when done.

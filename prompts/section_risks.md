Draft the **Key Risks** section of a diligence memo on {{target}}.

Work from the company's own Risk Factors disclosure, not from general knowledge of the
sector. For each risk you include:

- State it as the filing states it, in one or two sentences, with its evidence id.
- Say what in the business it touches — a segment, a supplier relationship, a geography — and
  cite that too where the filings connect them.
- Where a risk has a quantified exposure in the filings (a concentration percentage, a debt
  maturity, a legal reserve), include the figure with its id.

Prioritize by what would change a deal decision, not by the order the filing lists them.
Six to ten risks is the right length; a transcription of the whole Item 1A is not useful.

Risk Factors sections are written defensively and list many low-probability items. Say which
risks the filings themselves treat as material — for example by quantifying them or by
discussing them in MD&A as well — and mark the rest as boilerplate rather than including them
at equal weight.
